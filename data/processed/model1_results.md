# Model 1 - Course to Category classifier: results

_Generated by `notebooks/model1_classifier.ipynb` - do not edit by hand. Every figure here is also in `model1_results.json`._

**Task.** Supervised multi-class text classification, 11 categories, on the frozen stratified split. Input = course title + short intro. Selection rule: best **dev macro-F1** picks the model; **test** is scored exactly once.

- Split (seed 42): train 1805 / dev 386 / test 386 | classes 11
- Why macro-F1: classes are imbalanced (Business ~32%), so accuracy alone is misleading - a majority guesser scores ~0.32 accuracy but ~0.04 macro-F1.

**Selected: `tfidf-logreg`** (highest dev macro-F1). The MLP is the trained neural model whose training statistics appear below; LogReg is the baseline that edged it on macro-F1.
